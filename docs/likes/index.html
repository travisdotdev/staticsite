A bit about me

I'm a Computer Science and Business student at Trinity College Dublin. Away from code, music is a big part of my life. I taught piano for four years and now work as a Music Assistant and Supervisor for kids alongside my college work.

Most of my time at a computer is spent in a terminal, on a NixOS, Hyprland and Neovim setup I configured myself. An old laptop of mine stays on as a home server, currently running a Valheim server.

Reading and watching

  • The Grug Brained Developer: Carson Gross's essay, written in a caveman voice, on why complexity is the enemy and how to keep it out of your code
  • Molly Rocket: Casey Muratori's studio, home of Handmade Hero, his series building a game from scratch in C, and Computer Enhance, his course on how software performance really works
  • A calculator app? Anyone could make that.: the greatest calculator app development story ever told. Chad Nauseam on how Hans-J. Boehm made Android's calculator always show the correct answer, working up from floating point through rational numbers to constructive reals
